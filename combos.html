Silent Takedown

Quick Escape

Guard Distraction

Camera Bypass

Time movement between camera sweeps

Wait → Watch sweep → Move during blind spot

Double Takedown

Two guards together → Stun grenade → Double choke

G → Click → E → E (quick succession)

Laser Grid Dance

Time movements to weave through laser patterns

Watch pattern → Crouch → Roll → Stand → Repeat

Pressure Plate Skip

Use partner or body to trigger then dodge

Partner steps on → You dodge → Partner moves

Vault Crack Combo

Speed cracking without triggering silent alarm

Click sequence → Hold E → Click 3x → Release
