Role Guide

🥷 Thief

Play as a thief trying to steal items and escape without being caught.

Abilities:

  • Hide among NPCs to blend in
  • Move silently to avoid detection
  • Use smoke bombs to distract hunters
  • Dash ability for quick escapes

🔍 Hunter

Play as a hunter trying to find and eliminate the thieves.

Abilities:

  • Spot real players among NPCs
  • Track thief footprints
  • Deploy traps in key areas
  • Close range elimination attack

🎯 Role Tips

  • Thieves: Act like a bot to avoid suspicion
  • Thieves: Use NPCs as cover when hunters approach
  • Hunters: Watch for unusual movement patterns
  • Hunters: Check escape routes frequently
