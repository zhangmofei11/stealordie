Stealth Tips

🥷 Thief Tips

  • Move slowly near hunters - running draws attention
  • Watch NPC behavior patterns to mimic them
  • Use the environment - hide behind objects
  • Time your escapes when hunters are distracted
  • Stick to the edges of the map
  • Learn the spawn points to avoid early detection

🔍 Hunter Tips

  • Watch for unusual NPC movement - bots follow patterns
  • Check escape routes frequently
  • Use audio cues - footsteps give thieves away
  • Place traps at high-traffic areas
  • Work with other hunters to cover more ground
  • Don't stare at one spot - check everywhere

🎯 General Tips

  • Practice makes perfect - learn the maps
  • Use headphones for better audio awareness
  • Stay calm - panic leads to mistakes
  • Communicate with teammates if in co-op
  • Know when to give up and restart
