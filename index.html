ABOUT THE GAME

THE EXPERIENCE

Steal Or Die is a stealth-based hide-and-seek game on Roblox. Thieves must blend in with NPCs while stealing items, while Hunters try to identify and eliminate the real players.

KEY FEATURES

  • • Two team gameplay: Thieves vs Hunters
  • • Up to 16 players per match
  • • Stealth mechanics and item collection
  • • Unlockable skills and cosmetics
  • • Multiple maps and game modes
  • • Cross-platform play
